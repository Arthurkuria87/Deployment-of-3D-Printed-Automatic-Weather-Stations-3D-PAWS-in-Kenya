The 3D-PAWS Validation Toolkit was developed to provide a structured and reproducible software workflow for evaluating observations from the 3D-PAWS network against corresponding KMD reference observations. The toolkit supports station-level validation, network-level aggregation, statistical summarisation and generation of the results used in Chapter 7 of this study.
The workflow was designed to retain station-level differences rather than averaging them away. This allows the performance of individual stations and parameters to be examined while also providing an overall assessment of the network.
